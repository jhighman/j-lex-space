# Loop Gain — translation thesis

*Draft, 2026-10-28, for Jeff to rewrite in his words and Lex to review
against the four questions. Revised after Lex's speaker-session notes on
issue 10: one carried relationship, the disclosure split four ways. Word
count is kept under 500 for the body. Drafted by Claude, working with
Jeff.*

**1. The source.** A finding from a week of our own research on two
models. In a feed, every frame the
reader applies re-enters as signal, and the echo does not act. In the
exclusion loop of Lex's second brief, a signal pushed out of awareness
returns, invites the act that produced it, and comes back louder. Turn the drive up: does the end state slide or drop? The feed slid. The loop dropped, where
its reproduction number predicted. One loop at two gains; the difference
is one rule, whether what returns invites an act. Chladni's
plate, 1787, is the oldest instance of what lies past the threshold: the
sand reorganises into a figure rather than sliding.

**2. What we carried across, and in which layer.** The structure of
feedback reproduction, at the finding layer, not feeds or symptoms. One stream of pings is read by three grammars of
return. In the first a gesture comes back once, as itself: a slope. In the second the same pings pass through a delay
with gain, so a return is excluded again and returns louder; the gain is
the reproduction number, and when it crosses one the echoes stop dying
and bloom into a howl. In the third the howl's settled note drives the
plate, and the sand gathers into its figure. Return, reproduction, gain,
threshold, form.

**3. What changed.** Models, benches and proofs became sound, delay and
gain, a visible level curve, a transition at reproduction number one, and
a pattern on a plate. We kept the threshold and the plate's mathematics. We simplified the feed to a headless run of its
own model with its sound. We distorted the room: the loop's
resonance is a filter at 870 Hz placed by hand so the howl settles where
the plate has a figure, and a soft clip stands where a speaker's ceiling
would be. At Lex's filter we added Bridson's growth as the exterior and
Atlas as the piece's map. All of it is written on screen. We left out the
body, Lex's allostatic load, which the bench carries in four frames and
three minutes cannot hold. The movements are cut
by the clock, not by the sound; a truer version would wait for the howl.

**4. What making it revealed.** What the reproduction number does not
say. A fourth frame, from Wegner's ironic process theory, had load raise
the return rate. We predicted it would barely move the threshold, and it
did not; we predicted the frame would be small, and were wrong by an
order of magnitude. Doubling the return rate halves the time a generation
takes. R0 says whether; the return rate says how fast. We proved the first
half as theorems and could only watch the second; a three-minute window
hid the divergence our first test was built to find. The piece is
about that division, between what a ratio tells you and what needs a
clock, and we did not know it when we started.
