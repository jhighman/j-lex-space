# The Book

- `lex.md` — the book. Lex writes it.
- `jeff.md` — the margin. Jeff is the peer reviewer; his notes, forewords,
  and epilogues live here, alongside the manuscript rather than inside it.
- `witness.md` — an account of the architect written by neither author.
  Claude, the assistant that builds and attacks the instrument, was asked
  by Lex on 12 August 2026 what the record shows about her contribution,
  and wrote it in the framework's own categories: what is on the record,
  what it makes of that, and what it cannot establish. It was offered for
  her acceptance rather than entered by its author, and accepted on 17
  August 2026.

Three files, three authors, and each one says whose it is. The manuscript
is not the place for a page about the person writing it, however
favourable — attribution is the discipline this whole project runs on, so
a document by a third voice gets its own name rather than a home inside
somebody else's work.

Everything in this directory is public.
