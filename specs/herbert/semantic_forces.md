## 4. Ontological Triad: Grammatical Vectors as 3D Coordinates (The Emotion Premise)

Rather than contrasting acoustic phonetics (vowels) against action verbs, our architecture structures grammatical categories into exact topological dimensions:

1. **Nouns (Grounding / Centers):**
   - **Role:** Substantive anchors and invariant fulcrums in the ground plane $(X, Y)$.
   - **Function:** Provide the inertia and physical/epistemic entities. Without nouns, actions float in a vacuum without reference points.

2. **Verbs (Kinetic State Transitions):**
   - **Role:** Directional force vectors connecting nouns across $(X, Y)$.
   - **Function:** Drive transformation, tension, and trajectory. Without verbs, the system is in a frozen static state (*freeze*).

3. **Adjectives (Emotional Charge / Z-Axis Elevation):**
   - **Role:** Affective amplitude, intensity, and non-linear elevation along the $Z$-axis.
   - **Function:** Modulate the texture, resonance, and valence of the relationship. When an affective threshold is crossed, the adjective triggers non-linear displacement (via Rössler attractor dynamics), warping the 2D plane into a 3D orbital landscape.

> **Key Takeaway:** Semantic ambiguity (*innuendo*) and implicit charge do not operate on acoustic vowels alone; they emerge when flat 2D action paths $(X, Y)$ are displaced into affective topological depth $(Z)$ by the adjective's emotional vector.
