# Semantic Forces & Emotional Geometry Specification

## 1. Topological Shift: Mandala / Rosette Model
- **Center (Singularity / Gravitational Core):** God / Rest / The fulcrum of the pulley.
- **Perimeter (Temporal Orbit):** Human trajectory through linear verse and earthly time.
- **Action Mechanism:** Replacing linear phonetic recurrence with volumetric semantic chords pulling toward or away from the core.

## 2. Herbert's Principal Action Verbs: Semantic Classification & Forces
*Interpretive labels within the governing metaphysical frame:*

| Verb | Semantic Action | Proposed Force | Directional Character |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **pour** | Transfer blessings outward | Centrifugal | Expanding outward |
| **dispersed** | Spread across the world | Centrifugal | Scattering |
| **contract** | Gather into a span | Centripetal | Drawing inward |
| **flowed** | Release, circulation | Centrifugal | Fluid dissipation |
| **stay** | Arrest further distribution | Stabilizing | Fixed locus |
| **bestow** | Transfer possession | Centrifugal | Outward release |
| **adore** | Direct devotion toward gifts | False attraction | Worldly drift (off-axis) |
| **rest** | Settle into an apparent center | Competing equilibrium | False center vs. True center |
| **keep** | Retain without resolution | Containment | Potential energy |
| **lead** | Guide toward a destination | Centripetal | Vector path to center |
| **toss** | Propel through instability | Centripetal (at destination) | Locally disruptive, globally restorative |

> **Key Invariant:** The same verb can be locally disruptive and globally restorative. *Toss* functions as the primary mechanism of reunion.

## 3. Emotional Arc Specs
- **Joy / Happiness:** Kinetic resonance radiating symmetrically outward from the core without detachment.
- **Sorrow / Melancholy:** Heavy centripetal draw; high-density tension cords anchoring to the fulcrum ("Rest").
