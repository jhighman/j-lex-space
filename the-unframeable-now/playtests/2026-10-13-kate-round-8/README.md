# Kate, round 8 · 2:00 · a bad day · the thing at eight seconds · the report after

*Recorded: `kate-round-8-120s-bad-day.mp4` (15 MB, in this folder), with the sound rendered in. Debrief: `debrief.png`. The decay report at the end: `decay-report.png`. Hands off after 0:45.*

## The thing at eight seconds

I was two taps in. The pulse was going, the pings were coming in clumps, I'd opened the school portal card and was reaching for the garden one, and the screen went dark and a face came up out of it, screaming, full size, right in the middle, and a sound with it that I felt in my sternum. One second. Then gone, and the feed still scrolling underneath as if nothing had happened.

I said a word I don't say.

I want to be precise about what it did, because the game will be precise about it later. My hands came off the trackpad. My heart went. For about ten seconds I didn't do anything at all, and then I did the next things faster, which I noticed afterwards in the recording: the like at 0:09 is almost immediately after, and it's hurried.

Then I forgot it. That's the part that interests me. By 0:30 I was tapping an explainer about the rate decision as if a face hadn't screamed at me twenty seconds earlier. The feed had buried that too.

The card afterwards said: *At 0:08 something jumped at you. That was us, not the feed. You chose a bad day.* And I did choose it. The pace button said "things you didn't ask for." I'd read that and chosen it anyway, which is also exactly what I do with my phone.

**Does it belong?** I'm going to say what I'd say to a friend: I hated it, I wouldn't warn anyone off it, and it is the only moment in eight rounds where my body answered before I did. The game is about things arriving that you didn't ask for. For two minutes that was an idea. For one second it was true.

## The report

After the debrief there's a button, *See what it did to you*, and I pressed it expecting to be told off.

Three pictures. A disc that's torn. A grid that's half gone to grey rubble. Four lines falling across two minutes, with a dotted red one rising. Then I pressed replay and watched it happen again at six times speed: the disc whole, then a bite out of the top at 0:08 where attention and calm both dropped at once, the field going to noise in the first thirty seconds, the lines settling into their slopes after I stopped.

The sentence under the scrubber as it played: *0:08 · something you did not ask for.* Then *0:09 · you, liking the garden · since then, the weather.* The weather is a good word for it.

**What it told me, by the numbers:** attention 100 to 15. Calm 100 to 70, having been down near 30 at eight seconds. Two of my twenty minutes gone. Disorder from nothing to 0.42, 27 of 64 cells to noise.

## Is it useful?

The report is the most useful thing the game has done, and it has one lie in it.

**Useful:**
- *Attention 100 to 15.* I believe that. I could feel it, and the line shows it going down in steps, each step a tick, each tick a thing I did or a thing that arrived. Nothing in it is a judgment; it's a count.
- The bite at 0:08 in the disc. Calm and attention both notched at the same instant, and the replay shows it as a bite. That's what it felt like.
- The field. Watching the amber cells go grey one by one in the first half minute is the clearest picture of "my evening becoming noise" I've seen, and it's measured from the feed, not from me.
- *The minutes.* The line that only goes down, slowly, and never recovers. Two minutes gone. On a real night it's twenty, and that line would reach the floor, and I know it.

**The lie:** agency went back to 100%. I stopped at 0:45, my echoes aged out, the share of the feed that was me fell to nothing, and the model read that as my agency returning. It isn't. I didn't get my agency back by being buried. The green line rising while the blue one fell is the report congratulating me for giving up. Fix that. Agency should only come back when something I did actually worked, like the garden like did, and never because the feed outlasted me.

**One smaller thing:** disorder went *down* after I stopped, from the high 50s to 0.42, because fewer of my echoes were in the field. True by the definition, and the definition is stated. But it reads as the feed getting tidier when I leave it alone, which is the opposite of what the attention line says, and a person reading both at once gets two stories. Maybe disorder should include how much of what's in the field is under its cut-off *and still there*, so a field full of buried things reads as disordered even when it's quiet.

**Would I play again?** Not on a bad day. I've had the bad day. I'd play a quiet evening and watch the report to see if the disc can stay round. I don't think it can, and I think that's the point, but I want to see the shape of a good night.

**Verdict:** the report is the part I'd show someone. It drew the thing I couldn't say: that the evening wore down in steps, that each step had a cause, and that the one thing that only ever went one way was the time.
