The Unframeable Now · a two-minute experiment

Try to tidy a stream without leaving a trace of yourself in it.

What this is
A fake news stream with real feed machinery: a ranking, a cut-off, a lifetime, links between items. Nothing in it is real. The shape is.
What you do
Tidy it the way you tidy any feed: search, cut the clutter, size things up. The pads at the bottom are your tools.
What to watch
Time runs left to right. Every move you make is a violet mark above the stream, with an arc to where it comes back into the stream two seconds later. The band at the bottom is how much of the stream is you.
What you'll find
You can't keep the band flat. There is no outside to tidy from: every tidy-up, even a search, goes back in. Every real feed does this with your clicks, silently. Here it's drawn.
