The Unframeable Now · a two-minute experiment

Try to tidy a feed without leaving a trace of yourself in it.

What this is
A fake news feed with real feed machinery: a ranking, a cut-off, a refresh clock, “related” links. Nothing in it is real. The shape is.
What you do
Tidy it the way you tidy any feed: search, cut the clutter, change how you look at it. The pads at the bottom are your tools.
What you'll find
Every tidy-up comes back into the feed as one more item. You can't clean a stream from outside it, because there is no outside. A counter keeps score of your trace. It only goes up.
Why it matters
Every real feed does this with your clicks, silently. Here you can watch it happen, slowly, to you.
