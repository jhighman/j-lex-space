## Any channel (Lex, or anyone)

A visualizer that reads a poem over a song. Yesterday the poem supplied
the forces and the music supplied the feeling. Today the poem supplies
both: its nouns are the anchors the verbs act between, its adjectives
charge the body with joy or sorrow and hoist it up the rope, and the toss
at the end brings it home exactly. Built from a one-page spec in a day,
one test per claim, and every choice it forced is written on the screen
so the spec's author can say where it is wrong.
https://github.com/jhighman/j-lex-space
