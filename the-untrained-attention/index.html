The Untrained Attention

One attention head, two routes to the same map. On the left its weights are learned by gradient descent, step after step, against a loss. On the right the identical map is assembled by a short, fixed program with no data and no loss. The volume below holds both results so they can be laid over each other.

Learned Q and K start random; descend the loss

Gradient of the per-row KL divergence through the softmax, taken by hand (see gradientStep). Plain gradient descent, no momentum, no noise added.

Algorithmic simulator the same map, by construction

    No loss and no data: a fixed trace of elementary operations that ends at the target. In the paper the simulator is itself a transformer, so this is attention simulating attention.
