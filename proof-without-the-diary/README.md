# Proof Without the Diary

Part three of *Safeguards and Invariants*.

Part one asked nine questions of a machine a child confides in. Part two
ended on a promise: show that information did not cross a boundary, without
building a surveillance system to watch the boundary.

This is the payoff. It follows the argument from the child, to the small
engine built to answer it, to what that engine got wrong, and only then to
Europe — where the same question is being asked of a rulebook by thirteen
Member States who have not framed it this way.

The engine, the guards and the wordless game are in this repository and in
`jhighman/arapahoe` and `jhighman/milo-and-me`. The numbers quoted here
(ninety-nine refusals recorded as zero; 85,983 bytes where the screen claimed
none; three hand-written copies of one list drifting within a day) are from
those runs and are reproducible from them.
